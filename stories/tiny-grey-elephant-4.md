#Tiny Grey Elephant

I had just finished crocheting the small grey elephant for my nephew and was placing it in a gift bag when it let out a little trumpeting noise.

I was baffled. I immediately looked at my dog Spot, who had cocked their head in similar confusion. "There's no way," I said in disbelief. But when I tipped the giftbag out onto the coffee table a tiny fluffy element tumbled out.

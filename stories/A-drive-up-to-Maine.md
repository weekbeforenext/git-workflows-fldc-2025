# A Drive Up 2 Maine 🏎️
A road trip was just the thing for me and my friends.

While embarking on a scenic road trip from Florida to Maine, a group of friends experienced the unimaginable when a sudden, blinding light enveloped their car on a remote stretch of highway. In the blink of an eye, they found themselves aboard an alien spacecraft, surrounded by creatures from another world, curious about human life. After what seemed like hours of observation, they were gently returned to their car, with memories of the starry void and a story no one would believe.
